DIRECT SPONSORSHIP

A thoughtful partnership.
A clear boundary.

We’re opening Heldnote’s public pages to direct sponsorships. Placements are arranged with OFCode, clearly labelled, and served without third-party advertising scripts.

What is available?

A compact sponsor placement on the public landing page and selected guides. The editor is kept free of sponsor placements. There is no active sponsor today; availability, duration and pricing are agreed directly.

What would a placement include?

  • A clear “Sponsored” label.
  • Approved text and an optional image hosted with Heldnote’s own assets.
  • A normal sponsor link marked as sponsored.
  • Layout space reserved before the page loads, rather than an ad pushing content around.

What is outside the agreement?

No access to private notes, no targeting from note contents, no tracking pixels and no third-party scripts. We do not promise impression or click counts from tracking that does not exist. Any reporting must use an explicitly agreed approach that preserves these boundaries.

Who is a good fit?

Products and services relevant to writing, reading, learning and thoughtful work. We review the creative and destination before accepting a placement. Sponsorship is labelled commercial support, not an editorial recommendation.

AdSense comes later.

Programmatic ads are a separate project. They will need an isolated publishing origin, provider approval and appropriate consent and privacy disclosures. No AdSense code, account identifier or advertising cookie is enabled here.
