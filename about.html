Write freely. Return anytime.

Heldnote is a notepad that keeps every version of every note — in your browser, on your device. No account, no server, no setup.

Open Heldnote

Free · works offline · nothing to install

  • Every version, held

    The note as it was an hour ago, yesterday, last month — still there, one click away, restored without losing what you have now.

  • Yours, locally

    Notes live in your browser's own storage. They leave your device only if you back them up — to a file, or to your own Google Drive.

  • Honest about storage

    The status bar states two facts separately: what Heldnote has stored, and what your browser promises about keeping it. They are never blended into one reassuring green light.
