THE HELDNOTE IDEA

A small place for thoughts in progress.

Heldnote is a local-first browser notepad by OFCode. Its purpose is simple: make it easy to begin writing, change your mind, and return to earlier work.

By Ömer Faruk Bayrak / OFCode · Updated 24 October 2026

heldnote

Writing should begin before setup.

A quick thought should not need a workspace, a team, or an account. Heldnote starts with a plain writing surface, separate notes and local saving. The tools stay close enough to help, and quiet enough to let you think.

Your first draft deserves a way back.

Writing changes as you understand an idea. Version history is part of the notepad itself: a way to revisit a sentence, recover a detail and try again. Restoring an earlier version keeps the text you replace in history too.

Trust comes from being specific.

We say where the notes live: in this browser, on this device. We distinguish a successful save from the browser’s promise to retain data. We explain that history has limits, sync carries changes, and an exported backup is worth keeping.

Heldnote’s intention is to reduce accidental text loss. It is not a promise that a browser, device or cloud provider can never fail. Read how storage works before trusting any browser with your only copy.

A mark for holding onto a thought.

The folded H brings two upright strokes together around a small shared space. A terracotta fold marks the corner of a page — a small reminder that there is another version to return to. Forest green, warm paper and a readable serif keep the visual language calm and familiar.

Built simply, with a clear boundary.

Heldnote is plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, hosted on GitHub Pages. Local writing needs no account. Optional Google Drive sync talks directly to Google. The interface is available in English and Turkish.

The code is published for inspection, with all rights reserved. It is not offered under an open-source license.

How we think about advertising.

No advertising integration is active today. Future commercial support must be clearly labelled and must not use private note text for targeting. Third-party advertising scripts must be isolated from the origin that stores notes and Drive credentials. Adding a provider requires a separate review of placement and privacy disclosures.
