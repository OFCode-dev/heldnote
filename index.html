A few things
worth knowing.

Clear answers, before you start writing.

Do I need an account?

No. Local writing needs no account, email, or sign-up. Connecting Google Drive is optional and uses your own Google account.

Where are my notes stored?

In this browser’s IndexedDB storage, on this device. With Drive sync enabled, notes and version history also travel directly between your browser and your Google Drive app folder.

Can I use Heldnote offline?

You can keep editing an already loaded page while offline. Opening or reloading Heldnote without a connection is not guaranteed; the app currently has no offline-loading service worker.

Will I ever lose a note?

Version history and local autosave help you recover earlier writing, but no browser app can guarantee permanent storage. Clearing site data, private browsing, storage eviction, or device loss can remove notes. Keep exported backups.

Why does Google ask me to choose an account again?

Drive access tokens expire. Heldnote reuses a valid token and does not force repeat consent, but obtaining a new token may show Google’s account chooser. This is separate from saving your notes locally.
